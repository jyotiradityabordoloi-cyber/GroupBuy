Questions about group buys

What if the group does not fill?

The group buy closes at its time, your UPI app releases the hold, and nothing is paid.

Who can start a group buy?

Anyone living in the society. Pick a product from the list, set the closing time and share the link.

Where is my order delivered?

To the society gate only, never to each flat. The day and time are on every group buy.

Which UPI apps can I pay with?

Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm or any other app with a UPI ID.

Can I join more than one group buy?

Yes. Each one closes, is paid and is delivered on its own day.

Damaged items at delivery

Send a photo from the app on the day of delivery. The amount for that item goes back to the UPI ID you paid from.
